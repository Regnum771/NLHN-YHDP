# 01 — Scope and plan

The approved proposal describes the whole platform; its figures are illustrative. The IT team delivers incrementally. The spec's "R1" (January 2027 MVP) is split into **R1.0** (store release, December 2026) and **R1.1** (January–February 2027, before the March 2027 exam).

## Releases

| Release | When | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **R1.0** | Build 02/11–13/12/2026; store submission 16/12; live on stores by 31/12/2026 | Mobile app (iOS, Android): activation-code sign-in, consent, choose học phần, optional diagnostic; cited Q&A chat; học phần practice with MCQ quizzes and expert explanations ("Giải thích thêm" tutor); fixed mock exams (and the diagnostic as a fixed form); revision cards generated by rule from wrong answers with FSRS review (card review + simple deck list); Home and minimal Progress; error reporting. Web review workbench for experts; CSV user import; logging, monitoring, backups |
| **R1.1** | 01–02/2027 | Agent-built revision cards; freeform answer grading; review calendar; adaptive item selection in units; automatic blueprint exam assembly; deck browser and review settings; learner web app; usage dashboard and research export; coverage matrix and corpus library in the workbench; `calculate` tool for epidemiology formulas |
| **R2** | Build Q2–Q4/2027; integration Q1/2028; pilot Q2/2028 (≈250–300 learners) | Agent-proposed long-term study plan; local model hosting (7–8B / 13–15B / 24–32B tiers); expert escalation queue; lecturer portal and analytics; classes and exam scheduling; structured knowledge tables and knowledge graph; finite-state conversation manager |

## Store requirements to plan for

- Healthcare apps must be submitted by a legal entity (Apple); organisation accounts on both stores need a D-U-N-S number (can take up to ~30 days).
- Google Play: personal accounts created after 13/11/2023 need ≥ 12 testers for 14 consecutive days of closed testing before production access; organisation accounts are likely exempt but plan the 14-day beta anyway.
- Provide a demo account for store reviewers (sign-in is by activation code).
- In-app reporting of offensive / wrong AI content (Google Play AI-generated content policy) — covered by the report-error sheet.
- Clear "not a substitute for medical advice" statement; in-app account deletion path; public privacy-policy URL; Play Data safety form.
- Apple review may be slower around 23–27/12; submit by 16/12.
